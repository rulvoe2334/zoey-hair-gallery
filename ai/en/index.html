Lesson 1

Auto-reply to IG comments

Uses Meta's official, built-in, free feature — no third-party apps, no risk of getting your account banned.

What it looks like when you're done
AI
⚡ Auto-replyHey! Here's the full guide 👇
(whatever link you want to share)

Setup steps

  1. Log in at business.facebook.com → open "Inbox" in the left menuYour IG needs to be a professional account, linked to a Facebook Page
  2. Click "Automations" → create an automation → choose "Comment to message"
  3. Set your trigger keywordUse a single word — like AI, gallery, or price. Don't use a whole sentence
  4. Write two pieces of text: the DM (your link + one open question) and the public replyThe open question matters: once they reply, it becomes a real conversation — and the algorithm rewards that
  5. Turn it on, then test it once with a second account
People often can't find the DM — it lands in their "Message requests" folder. In your public reply, add "check your message requests if you don't see it."

Lesson 2

AI writes your captions & scripts

Any of these tools works, and the free tier is enough. The tool isn't the point — how you brief it is.

Claude (what I use) ChatGPT Gemini

Copy this prompt (example: a new-work post)

You are my IG copywriter. I'm a hair stylist; most of my clients are women aged 25–45 with office jobs. Write 3 versions of an IG caption for my latest work: - The hair: milk-tea brown, shoulder-length cut (swap in yours) - The client's worry: warm skin tone, fine/thin hair (swap in yours) - My voice: like chatting with a friend — no salesy words, no "hey babes" - Always end with: Comment "gallery" and I'll DM you my portfolio Keep each under 80 words, and give me 3 different opening hooks first.
Paste your 2 best past posts into the chat so it "learns your voice" — the output will sound more and more like you.
✓ After this lesson, a caption takes 5 minutes instead of 40
Lesson 3

Auto-captions & cutting filler words

Two routes. Start with the phone route — only consider the computer route after 10+ videos.

Phone route (free · fastest to learn)

  1. CapCut → import your video → "Auto captions"Recognition is very accurate — you'll only fix 2–3 words by hand
  2. Listen through once and delete every chunk of "so, um, like, you know…"Cutting filler words instantly tightens the whole video's pacing
  3. Don't use the default caption style: white text + dark outline, with keywords in one consistent brand color

Computer route (how I made my video)

Use Whisper (free, open-source) to transcribe the video, hand the transcript to AI to flag filler words and dead air with timestamps, then auto-export the final cut — even the caption animations were laid out by AI. This route takes some computer skills; if you want to learn it, tell me on IG — if enough people ask, I'll write the next guide.

The voiceover in my video is also AI, generated with my own voice (it runs locally — my voice never gets uploaded). That's an advanced topic — comment "voice" if you want me to cover it.

FAQ

Does any of this cost money?

Everything on this page is completely free: Meta's auto-reply is built into their official tools, and the free tiers of the AI tools and CapCut are plenty. Once it becomes a habit, you can think about paid plans — no rush.

Will auto-replies get my IG account banned?

No. This is an official feature inside Meta's own dashboard — completely different from third-party plugins. What you should be careful with are sketchy third-party tools: never casually authorize them on your account.

I'm terrible with computers. Can I really do this?

Lessons 1 and 2 can be done entirely on your phone. I can't code either — I get everything done by "talking" to AI. The only skill you need to practice is describing what you want, clearly.
